共 19 张独立 PNG，保存在 `dist/assets/scenes/`，对应关系见 `dist/scene-backgrounds.js`。内置 image_gen 生成，未使用 CLI。所有画作已接入对应剧情，背景不包含人物。夜景直接使用原画光线，不重复叠加暗色滤镜。

## 共用画风提示

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a distinct background painting for one scene of a Sichuan University design-freshman visual novel. Treat supplied campus image as the STRICT STYLE MASTER, not a composition to copy. Match its authentic refined pixel art: visible crisp stepped pixel clusters, clean dark-mid outlines where needed, flat limited-color shading, detailed but readable environmental textures, warm cream/ochre/leaf-green palette and soft blue sky. Same calm cozy campus world and pixel resolution across every image; not smooth anime painting, vector art, 3D or a photographic pixel filter. Architecture is fictional narrative scenery inspired by the game's established campus, not a claim of a surveyed real building. ONE continuous full-frame landscape 16:9 environment, roughly 1536x864; fully opaque, edge-to-edge. No borders, panels, captions, lettering, readable signs, logos, watermarks or UI. Absolutely NO PEOPLE, faces, silhouettes, crowds or characters: character sprites are overlaid later. Eye-level camera around 1.5m height, natural human-scale perspective; no overhead or extreme wide-angle views. Stage a plausible open foreground/right-center area for a full-body character, furniture in midground; leave low-contrast calm space at top and bottom for game overlays.
